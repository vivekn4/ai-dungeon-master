## Sound effects

All SFX are **synthesized live** by the original zero-asset WebAudio engine in `js/sfx.js` — no audio files ship with the repo, so there is nothing to license. (Kenney.nl CC0 packs were evaluated for signature moments like the dice roll and fanfare; the tested synth engine was kept instead to guarantee the repo works offline with zero binary weight.)

## Visual effects

`js/vfx.js` is an original canvas particle engine (ambient embers, confetti, sparkles, screen shake) — no third-party code.
